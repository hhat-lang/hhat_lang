# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the H-hat community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

* **Being respectful**: Using welcoming and inclusive language
* **Being empathetic**: Showing empathy towards other community members
* **Being constructive**: Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* **Being accountable**: Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
* **Being community-focused**: Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community
* **Being helpful**: Supporting newcomers and helping others learn

### Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* **Harassment**: The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* **Trolling**: Insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, and sustained disruption
* **Privacy violations**: Publishing others' private information, such as physical or email addresses, without explicit permission
* **Professional misconduct**: Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting
* **Discrimination**: Any form of discrimination based on the characteristics mentioned in our pledge

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including but not limited to:

* GitHub repositories (issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews)
* Discord server (`#h-hat` channel and others)
* Email communications
* Social media interactions
* Community events (online or in-person)
* Any other forums where community members represent the H-hat project

This Code of Conduct also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders and maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Reporting Guidelines

### How to Report

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns, please report it by:

1. **Discord**: Contact a moderator using `@moderator` or send a direct message
2. **Email**: Contact the Unitary Foundation at [conduct@unitary.foundation]
3. **GitHub**: For public repository issues, mention `@hhat-lang/moderators`

### What to Include

When reporting, please include:

* Your contact information
* Names (usernames) of people involved
* Description of the incident
* When and where it occurred
* Any additional context or evidence
* Whether you've reported this elsewhere

All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality.

### Response Process

1. **Acknowledgment**: Within 48 hours, you'll receive confirmation that your report was received
2. **Review**: Moderators will review the incident and gather additional information if needed
3. **Decision**: The moderation team will decide on appropriate action
4. **Communication**: You'll be informed of the outcome (within reasonable privacy constraints)
5. **Appeal**: You may appeal a decision by contacting the Unitary Foundation

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these guidelines in determining consequences:

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other unprofessional behavior.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved for a specified period. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces and external channels. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period. No public or private interaction with the people involved is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any public interaction within the community.

## Additional Guidelines

### Inclusive Language

* Use gender-neutral language when addressing groups
* Respect people's pronouns and names
* Avoid assumptions about backgrounds or experiences
* Be mindful of cultural differences

### Technical Discussions

* Critique ideas, not people
* Be patient with beginners
* Share knowledge freely
* Give credit where it's due
* Accept that there are multiple valid approaches

### Conflict Resolution

When disagreements arise:

1. **Assume good intent**: Most conflicts arise from miscommunication
2. **Communicate clearly**: Express your perspective calmly and specifically
3. **Listen actively**: Try to understand the other person's viewpoint
4. **Find common ground**: Focus on shared goals
5. **Escalate if needed**: Involve moderators if you can't resolve it

### Privacy

* Don't share others' personal information without consent
* Be cautious with screenshots that include usernames
* Respect confidentiality when explicitly requested
* Consider anonymizing examples when sharing problems
